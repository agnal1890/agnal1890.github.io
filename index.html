Featured project

Telecom customer churn

243,553 customer records · Python, Pandas, Scikit-learn · 2026

The most accurate model caught none of the customers who left.

About 1 in 5 customers in this dataset churned (20.05%). A model that simply predicts "nobody leaves" is already 80% accurate, so accuracy says very little here. What matters is recall: of the customers who actually left, how many did the model catch?

Digging further, every model's precision sat at about 20%, the same as the churn rate itself, and every feature had near-zero correlation with churn. My conclusion: this dataset carries almost no signal about who will leave, and a model built on it shouldn't be deployed. Knowing when the data can't answer the question is part of the job.
